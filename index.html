1. how to use

  1. Draw: pick a character on the left and draw it on the canvas over the faint letter.
  2. Check: type in PREVIEW.TXT to see your font.
  3. Download: give it a name and hit "Download .otf".

2. install

Computer: double-click the .otf file and choose "Install".

Canva: upload the .otf under Brand Kit → Fonts (may need a paid plan).

3. privacy

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Fonts you draw belong to you.
